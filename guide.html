✦ Quick start guide
Getting started
📲

Add to your home screen

Open the link in your browser → tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Works on iOS and Android — no App Store needed.

How to use it
1

Set up your platforms

On your first login, Lumis Carte will ask what you collect — JP singles, EN slabs, a bit of everything. Based on your answer, it pre-selects the most relevant platforms for you. You can adjust anytime in Settings.

💡 Choose up to 3 platforms — keeps your checker clean and focused
2

Enter the card details

Type the card name, set, and grade. Look up recent sold prices on your active platforms and enter them in — the app averages and converts everything automatically.

💡 Tap the shortcut buttons next to each platform name to open them directly 💡 Optionally tell the app how often you see this card — it will help to provide some context and advise you accordingly
3

Pick your mode and enter the price

Tap I'm buying, I'm selling, or Auction, key in the price, and hit Check. The app instantly shows your signal and a commentary line tailored to the situation.

4

Save and move on

Add an optional note, then tap Clear & Save to History to log the check and start fresh.

💡 You can also Save to Watchlist before checking — great for prepping before a card show
The modes
🛒

I'm buying

Evaluating a listing or card show purchase. Enter price in SGD.

🏪

I'm selling

A vendor is making you an offer. See if it's fair vs selling independently.

🎙️

Auction

Sourcing from live stream auctions. Enter bid in SGD or CNY — enable in Settings.

💡 Auction mode supports both SGD and CNY bids — set your preferred default in Settings.

Reading the signal
✓

Good buy — at or below your threshold

You're buying below market. Pull the trigger with confidence.

~

Borderline — between threshold and 95%

Reasonably priced but not a steal. Your call — the app will help you think it through.

↑

Above market — above 95%

Paying at or above market rate. Not always wrong — but worth a moment of reflection.

On borderline or above market signals, the app may ask you a follow-up question. It's optional — but worth answering if you're struggling to decide.

A few tips
📋

Before a card show: Save cards you're hunting to your Watchlist at home with prices pre-filled. At the table, tap Load → enter the vendor price → done in seconds.

⚡

At a live auction: Eyeball the average from recent sales and enter one number per platform instead of all 5. Faster when the bidding heats up.

🔀

Check all platforms: Toggle between your active platforms — ALT, SNKRD, YYT, PC, and TCGP — prices can differ significantly for the same card across markets. Enable or disable platforms in Settings anytime.

👁️

Collapsed input bar: Once you've entered a price and collapsed the panel, the bar shows your entered price at a glance — so you always know what you're checking against.

👑

Chasing something special: Set a Grail % in Settings for cards you'd stretch your budget for. It activates per check — your regular threshold stays untouched.
